hillGPX: hills and free GPX routes

hillGPX is a free, open-source map of more than 900,000 hills and mountains in 232 countries, plus Singapore's stairwells and tall blocks. Runners and hikers share GPX routes up them, and every route is free to download with its real elevation gain and loss.

What you can do

  • Search any country, hill or town and see the climbs nearby on a map.
  • Open a route for its trace and elevation profile, and download the GPX.
  • Add your own GPX, photos and reviews. No account needed.
